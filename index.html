CommonRoom

A shared classroom on the web. Walk around as your character, chat, and talk with whoever is near you.

You will get a room code and an invite link to share. Keep this tab open during class; the room closes when you leave.

Closed your tab by accident? Reopen the same room and your students will reconnect on their own.

Try the 3D version (beta). 2D and 3D users can be in the same room.
